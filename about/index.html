About KontoMate

KontoMate is a read-only wealth tracker. It shows your bank accounts, investments, crypto, gold and cash in one view, with budgets, goals and an AI assistant that knows your numbers. It can never move your money.

Updated: 21.11.2026

What KontoMate does

  • Bank accounts: you import statements as a file, PDF or photo. KontoMate never asks for your online-banking login and has no connection to your bank.
  • Investments and crypto: Trading 212, Interactive Brokers, eToro, Binance, Kraken, Coinbase and Bybit, each connected with your own read-only API key. Keys that can trade or withdraw are refused.
  • Gold, silver and cash: gold and silver by the gram at today's price, and cash you add by hand.
  • Home and car: monthly costs, rent received and date reminders.
  • Money tools: a budget and an income and expense plan, goals and recurring investments, subscriptions found for you, price and budget alerts, and a monthly report.
  • AI assistant: ask about your own numbers in words or with a photo. It states facts and never gives investment advice.
  • Languages: English, Bulgarian, German, Spanish, French, Italian, Polish and Romanian. Amounts are in euro.

What KontoMate is not

KontoMate is a tracker. It is not a bank, a broker, an investment adviser or a wealth manager. There is no button to buy, sell, pay or transfer, and there never will be. Nothing in KontoMate or on this website is investment advice.
