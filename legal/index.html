What KontoMate is

KontoMate is a personal finance tracker. It shows your bank accounts, investments, crypto, gold, silver, cash, home and car in one place, with budgets, goals, alerts, reports and AI features. It only reads data: it cannot pay, transfer, trade or withdraw money. It gives no investment, tax or legal advice.

Where the data comes from

Bank accounts: KontoMate has no connection to your bank and never asks for your online-banking login. You import a statement (CSV or PDF), upload a photo or screenshot of a statement that AI reads for you, or type the data. Nothing from a file is saved until you have checked and confirmed every row, and the file itself is not kept.

Brokers and crypto exchanges (for example Trading 212, Interactive Brokers, eToro, Binance, Kraken, Coinbase, Bybit): you connect them with your own read-only API key. We store it encrypted, use it only to read, and refuse keys that could trade or withdraw.

Gold, silver, cash, home and car: entered by hand or from PDFs you import.
