    Questions

    When can I sign up?

    KontoMate opens soon. Leave your e-mail and we'll write to you once, when sign-up opens. Until then, the demo shows the whole app.

    Can KontoMate move my money?

    No. It only reads balances, transactions and holdings. It has no way to pay, transfer, buy or sell, and keys that could do so are refused.

    Is it free?

    Yes, the Free plan stays free. Pro costs €8 a month or €80 a year, and every account starts with 14 days of Pro without a card.

    Is it secure?

    We never ask for your bank login: bank data comes from the statements you import. Platform keys and 2FA secrets are stored encrypted. You can turn on two-factor login, you are logged out after 15 minutes without activity, and a login from a new browser sends you an alert.

    Which banks and platforms work?

    Any bank: import its statement as a file, a PDF or a photo. Platforms with a read-only key: Binance, Trading 212, Interactive Brokers, Kraken, Coinbase, Bybit and eToro. Anything else you can add by hand.

    Does it tell me what to invest in?

    No. KontoMate shows facts and trends about your own money. It is not investment advice and never recommends buying or selling anything.

    Can I take my data out, or delete it?

    Yes. Download everything as a file, delete all financial data, or delete the account completely from Settings.

    What happens in the demo?

    You use the real app with sample data. It runs entirely in your browser: nothing you type is sent to us, and it is gone when you reload.
